嫦娥奔月

The Legend of Chang'e

A timeless story told beneath the full moon.

嫦娥奔月 · The Moon Palace

English

Long ago, ten suns appeared in the sky and scorched the earth. The heroic archer Hou Yi shot down nine of them and saved the people. He was given an elixir of immortality and entrusted it to his wife, Chang'e.

When someone tried to steal the elixir, Chang'e drank it to keep it from falling into the wrong hands. She rose toward the heavens and chose the moon as her home. Since then, people have looked at the moon during Mid-Autumn Festival and remembered the people they love.

中文 · Mandarin

很久以前，天空中出现了十个太阳，大地被烈日炙烤。英雄后羿射下九个太阳，拯救了百姓。后来，他得到长生不老药，并把仙药交给妻子嫦娥保管。

当有人企图抢走仙药时，嫦娥为了不让仙药落入坏人手中，吞下了仙药。她飞向天空，最后住在月亮上。从此，人们在中秋节仰望明月，寄托对亲人的思念。
